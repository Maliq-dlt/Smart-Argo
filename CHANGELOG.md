# Changelog

Notable changes to Smart Agro. Dates are UTC+7.

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- Moving a device to another pot no longer stalls it: ingest files a packet under the unit the device was placed in at `measured_at` (migration `20261121000000`), so the flashed `unitId` only applies while unplaced. Before, a move answered `unit_mismatch` 403, which firmware retried forever. Packets for an archived unit are a permanent `unit_archived` 409. Android lets the owner move a placed device and archive a unit that still holds one, with a confirmation that says where the next readings go; a device that has not reported yet is a warning, not a refusal.
- ESP32 firmware sends the newest sample before the backlog after an outage, so the app shows a current reading at once.
- Android: no 8-second card auto-rotation, sensor metrics and headers survive 360 dp and 200% text, practice charts use the data range, a +5 minute clock margin like ingest, and probe errors from firmware become hints in Sensor diagnostics.

### Added
- Account deletion with a 14-day grace period (migration `20261122000000`, local only until approved for the cloud): asking needs a sign-in from the last ten minutes or a fresh authenticator code, releases the account's products, cancels its transfer offers and signs every session out. Until the purge every function but `my_account_deletion` and `cancel_account_deletion` refuses the account; cancelling gives back the products nobody claimed meanwhile. `purge_due_account_deletions` (service role) deletes gardens, plant cycles, care notes, assistant questions, the photo, the sign-in log and the GoTrue user, and keeps the account row as a tombstone so ledgers and ownership history stay consistent. Web: four steps at `#hapus-akun` (also reachable signed out) and a "dijadwalkan dihapus" page on sign-in; Android: the same in Akun › Data & privasi.
- Profile photo: the centre square at 256 px as WebP, at most 256 kB with magic-byte checks, readable only by its owner (`set_my_avatar`, `my_avatar`, `clear_my_avatar`). Web Profil and the account menu, Android Profil (Photo Picker). Google accounts show their Google photo until they pick one; `img-src` allows `lh3.googleusercontent.com` for that.
- Android "pot as the interface": the cut-away pot is the plant's header, one action card with five seconds to undo a watering note, measuring tapes and a day ruler, a home grid of mini pots, and Aktivitas with thin-bar charts and the journal by day.
- Battery voltage: firmware reads the RAB 47k/47k divider on GPIO32 into an optional `battery` object; `my_unit_overview` reports the latest voltage and raises `battery_low` below 3.4 V (migration `20261121010000`); Android diagnostics and the web unit list show it.
- Wi-Fi without USB: a WPA2 setup network `SmartAgro-xxxx` (EN, then hold BOOT for 2 seconds) stores the home network in NVS; `provision.mjs` prints the label password.
- Energy evidence: two INA228 meters (solar and load) feed 15-minute UTC windows to `energy-ingest`, signed with a separate meter key; the node wakes on the quarter-hour and keeps Wi-Fi to every 30 minutes.
- The first page no longer downloads framer-motion or the ecosystem engine: `MotionConfig` moved into the simulator, the landing reads reduced motion itself, React has its own chunk (the old `motion` manual chunk had pulled React in, so the landing preloaded all of framer-motion), and solar sizing is its own module. Landing JavaScript went from 579 kB to 425 kB (gzip 200 kB to 149 kB).
- `SECURITY.md` matched the code again: password reset, email codes, recovery codes, issuer signature checks, gardens, community data and weather were missing or described as absent.
- `tests/engine.test.ts` finds `python3` as well as `python`.

### Changed
- Dashboard redesigned around the answer. The stage has a sky that follows the session clock (sun, moon and a chili constellation at night, per theme), the clock sits at its foot labelled "Waktu simulasi", and the pot title is a small label so the engine's advice is the first read, also on a phone. The advice card keeps at most three facts and no longer repeats the stage's numbers.
- Moisture now has a condition band: three zones from the profile's own watering and drained limits, the line broken wherever a reading failed, a 24-hour ribbon, and values readable by pointer, tap or keyboard (←/→, Shift for 6 hours, Home/End, Esc), spoken through a live region. It replaces the lime trend card.
- Three instruments add context instead of repeating readings: growth phase against the 65/90-day journey, 24 hours of soil temperature against the heat limit, and the last three pH readings against the profile's range. Wording follows the product: "Hari ke-N" instead of "HST", "batas siram/panas" instead of "acuan", and the session pill says "Simulasi".
- The rail shows a word under each icon on desktop (the phone dock stays icon-only).
- Pure dashboard logic (zones, sun and moon, phase steps, "ago" labels) lives in `src/lib/dashboardFacts.ts`; `tests/dashboardFacts.test.ts` keeps the sky periods equal to the simulator scene's and the phase steps equal to `phaseAt`.
- Plant catalog (`src/lib/species.ts`) replaces the `onion` flag and the `'cabai' | 'bawang'` literals. One table per curated species holds the scenario it opens, the illustration's growth days, the last day of the journey and the reference phases, so the engine, simulator, session store, dashboard and phase guide no longer keep their own copies. Nothing visible changes (the rendered DOM is identical before and after for both species on every phase boundary), and `cabai`/`bawang` stay the stored ids, so tokens and sessions saved earlier still read back. Anggrek bulan and peoni are catalogued as learning-only species and are not yet selectable. `tests/phases.test.ts` checks the phases against a copy of the old functions.

- The pot stage is redrawn (`src/components/plantScene/`, ported from the Fase 2.0 mock): the pot in section with layered media and roots, the Smart Agro device drawn small and flat like the plants (squircle body, solar panel, skirt, one LED, two short probes), a sky that follows the session clock (sun, moon, stars, one colour matrix for night), and the three readings written beside the part they describe, with no boxes or dots. The plant droops when the media is dry and recovers when it is watered; watering plays a can, a stream and a water front, and the new moisture arrives with the water. One gentle breeze moves the whole plant and the pointer moves nothing. Springs replace CSS keyframes, and reduced motion lands every spring at once and skips the pour. It replaces `PlantIllustration`, `SkyScene`, the lens and the callouts, on the dashboard and in Ruang tanam alike. `tests/plantScene.test.ts` (clock through midnight, palette, springs, bands) and `tests/plant-scene-browser.mjs` (readings in frame at 1440/768/390/320 in both themes, no pointer listeners, night darker than noon, text at 200%, pour and reduced motion) cover it.

### Added
- Real weather (Fase 8, step 3): a signed-in person's outdoor pot or garden with coordinates shows the weather there, from Open-Meteo through the `weather-forecast` function, credited on the stage; everyone else keeps the simulated sky. The function now answers only a live session (an unknown bearer gets 401, an Auth outage 503 so the browser does not sign out).
- The live app (Fase 8, step 4) is published by the deploy workflow as the `live` branch of the Pages project, at `live.smart-agro-demo.pages.dev`, on the same hosted backend. Functions accept a comma-separated `APP_ORIGIN` list (`supabase/functions/_shared/origin.ts`).
- Issuer key (Fase 8, step 5): `scripts/issuer.mjs` makes the issuer key pair and signs product manifests; the public key `smart-agro-2026` is pinned in `.env.cloud` for the browser and in `ISSUER_PUBLIC_KEYS` for `product-provision`.
- `tests/schema-audit.integration.mjs` (in `npm run test:backend`) fails CI when a table in `public` lacks row-level security, `anon` holds a table privilege, `authenticated` can write a table or read one without a policy, a view runs with its owner's rights, or `anon` can run a function other than `product_public_status`.
- `tests/cloud-smoke.mjs` also checks the real Google client (Auth redirects to `accounts.google.com` with a client id and its callback), the weather function, and the live app.

- A signed-in person's pots and gardens now live on their account (Fase 8, step 2), so another device or browser shows the same garden. On sign-in the app reads `my_garden()` and keeps a copy on the device to show at once; which simulator session each slot opens and which slot is chosen stay the device's own and are carried over by slot id. Adding a pot or garden, replacing a plant and the first pot from "Siapkan pot" are written to the server first (each with its own request id, so a double click is one pot) and then read back. A server answer passes the same checks as stored data, so a broken one changes nothing. A guest's garden stays on the device as before, and "Muat contoh kebun" is offered to guests only. Pots that an account had only on this device before this change are kept under `smart-agro.garden.v1.<account>.device` and not uploaded. Plant codes are shown as two species letters and the number ("Cabai 1 · CB-1"), letters per species in the catalog (`tag`) and unique; the stored identity is still the integer. `tests/gardenServer.test.ts` and `tests/garden-sync-browser.mjs` (two devices against the local backend, checked in the database) cover it.
- The hosted project now has the garden, plant-quota and community migrations (Fase 8, step 1), after a schema and data backup. Community moisture bands are published to the nearest 5 points (migration `20261116000000`): a low-cost soil probe cannot tell nearer values apart, and an odd-sized median no longer equals one contributor's band. The two trigger functions without a fixed search path, which the hosted security advisor flagged, now have one (migration `20261116010000`); `tests/garden.integration.mjs` checks that no function in `public` lacks it.
- Community knowledge on the server (migration `20261115110000_community_knowledge.sql`, local database and repository only; the hosted project is unchanged and the app does not call it yet). Sharing is off until the owner of a slot turns it on, and turning it off takes back everything already shared from it. A finished plant can be shared once, and only if it grew well, for at least 30 days by the dates the owner gave and for at least 30 days by the server's own record of it (a planting date can be backdated, the server's clock cannot), in a slot that is opted in; what is shared is three numbers: the watering interval and the moisture band. The place is reduced to a coarse climate zone (indoor, unknown, tropical, subtropical, temperate, cold). Who shared it is kept only to count different owners and to withdraw a share, and is never returned. Signed-in users read `community_knowledge(species)`: one owner counts for at most two plants per zone, plants a curator excluded are left out, plants far outside the usual spread are trimmed, and only then is the threshold of five plants from three different owners applied; a zone below it is not listed and no count is given. The result is labelled "belum dikurasi". Curators with a verified authenticator list the shared plants (without account, slot or plant ids) and exclude or restore one with a reason. `tests/community.integration.mjs` (11 checks, part of `npm run test:backend`) covers all of this on synthetic data, including backdating, repeated replacing, one person sharing many plants, outliers, opting out in the middle of a plant's life, and that nothing identifying comes back to another user, to a curator listing or to the aggregate. Known limit: the interquartile trim holds only while about a quarter of the data is bad, so a patient person with three accounts could dominate a thin zone; time, the curators and the label are the defence, not a guarantee.
- Plant numbers are one shared supply, so an account can now open at most 300 plants and 30 in a day, and a species whose numbers have run out answers `limit_reached` instead of a database error (migration `20261115103000_garden_cycle_quota.sql`, found by the automated review of the garden migration).
- Pots, gardens and plant cycles on the server (migration `20261115100000_garden_sites_cycles.sql`, local database and repository only; the hosted project is unchanged). Tables for sites, slots, cycles, species and the plant-number counter are closed to every role but the service role; a signed-in user reaches their own data only through `my_garden`, `add_my_site`, `plant_in_my_slot` and `switch_my_plant`, which lock their search path, refuse a caller who is not signed in, and answer a retry of the same request with the first answer. Replacing a plant closes the old cycle and opens the new one in one step; a closed cycle can never change and no cycle is ever deleted (a trigger). A plant's number is taken under a row lock so no two plants of a species share one, its code is `species * 1_000_000 + number`, a place is kept to two decimals (about a kilometre) and not at all indoors, and a person has at most 20 pots and gardens and 60 slots. `tests/garden.integration.mjs` (13 checks against the local backend, now part of `npm run test:backend`) covers closed tables, isolation between owners, retries, validation, the immutable history, twelve concurrent plantings getting twelve different numbers, and the limits.
- Live mode: `npm run build:live` (`VITE_APP_MODE=live` from `.env.live`) builds the app a real user signs in to. It has no guest visit, no Ruang tanam (its address and the default page lead to the dashboard), no simulated weather and no demo garden. The mode is chosen at build time only; any other value is the demo. `tests/liveMode.test.ts` and `tests/live-mode-browser.mjs` (a live build served apart, sign-in scripted) cover it.
- Learning mode for a plant with no curated profile (the orchid in the demo garden). Its answer card becomes "Sedang belajar": it says it has no reference yet and gives no watering advice, shows how much it has seen (days watched, checks, waterings whose effect was measured, each against its threshold of 7 days, 3 checks and 2 waterings), and names the one thing to do now. The owner records a check ("Sehat", "Biasa", "Bermasalah"), kept with the session. Once every threshold is met the card turns to "Pola pot Anda" and states, always labelled "Berdasarkan pola pot Anda, bukan standar tanaman", only what was measured in this pot: the moisture range in the six hours after a check that found the plant well, the daytime drying rate and the rise after a watering. `src/lib/observation.ts` computes these by plain arithmetic (synthetic series in the tests, invalid readings ignored, same input same output). `src/lib/learningExplanation.ts` holds the wording and the prompt `smart-agro.explain.v2` for a model to rephrase at level 1, with a checker that refuses a dose, a number that is not in the evidence, a claim about what is best for the species, a missing origin, a link or markup, and falls back to the rules' own text; level 0 never calls a model. The demo has no model endpoint, so the rules' text is what shows. Moisture zones and temperature alerts from a curated profile are not drawn for such a plant.
- Pots and gardens on one dashboard. The pot pill in the top bar opens a picker of the sites and slots saved on this device (a personal pot is one site with one slot; a garden has a slot for each zone), with "Ganti tanaman", "Tambah pot atau kebun" and, while there is nothing saved, "Muat contoh kebun" (an orchid pot indoors and a garden of two zones). The wizard asks for the kind, name, placement (dalam ruangan, luar ruangan, atap; a location only outdoors), the plant found by typing part of its name, an alias or its Latin name, and the planting date (an earlier date has the simulator grow the plant to today, at most 60 days). A garden shows its zones above the stage, one tap to open each; a pot indoors has no weather control. Replacing a plant explains first that the old cycle closes and its history is kept, asks how it ended (berhasil, gagal, dihentikan), and starts the new plant at day 0; older cycles are never rewritten. Each plant has a code integer (`species * 1_000_000 + number`) and, unless named, a name like "Cabai 2". Saved per account (or `guest`) as `smart-agro.garden.v1.<scope>`; a damaged save is set aside as `.damaged`, never overwritten. `src/lib/garden.ts`, `gardenStore.ts`, `gardenSessions.ts`, `tests/garden*.test.ts` and `tests/garden-browser.mjs` cover it. The first pot an account sets up (`PotSetup`) is also its first slot.
- Learning species (anggrek, peoni) are in the catalog with search aliases, Latin names, a drawing (or none: only species with a drawing can be planted, so anggrek yes and peoni not yet) and a journey of one phase, "Mengamati". Their sessions use the simulator's synthetic readings with `validated: false`, so the engine holds back watering advice ("Tetapkan kembali acuan pot") instead of presenting a curated profile that does not exist.
- Simulated weather for the pot stage: a "Cuaca simulasi" control (Cerah, Berawan, Mendung, Hujan, Terik) on the dashboard, remembered in this browser (`smart-agro.weather`). Clouds gather and the sky and hills dim, rain falls in streaks, a hot day brightens the sun, warms the sky and shimmers the air, and the breeze strengthens or eases; reduced motion draws the rain still. A line under the stage mentions only rain timing or heat ("Sedang hujan: pertimbangkan menunda siram", "Suhu tinggi: periksa media lebih sering"), says nothing for an indoor pot, quotes only hours that are in the forecast and never an amount. `src/lib/weather.ts` holds the snapshot model, the five simulated skies and a pure Open-Meteo adapter (unknown WMO codes read as cloudy). `supabase/functions/weather-forecast/` is a handler for a real forecast (CORS locked to the app origin, place validated and rounded to about a kilometre, only the place sent to the provider, provider trouble becomes `weather_unavailable`); it is **not deployed**, not in `config.toml`, no deploy script and not in the local gateway. `docs/cuaca.md` records each provider's terms.
- Sign-up asks for a name and a confirmation of the password, then verifies the email with a six-digit code (also reachable by signing in with an unconfirmed email, with a send-again cooldown). The display name is editable in Profil and is not overwritten by the Google name.
- Signing up with an address that already has an account no longer waits for an email that never comes: the card moves to "Masuk" with the address filled in and says why (hosted Auth answers such a sign-up with an empty user; local Auth with an error).
- The sign-in card never jumps: tabs open and close the sign-up fields in place, the card is anchored at its top, a notice opens inside it, a new view rises in while the card eases to its height, and "Coba tanpa akun" and a finished sign-in leave the gate behind the loading screen. Password and confirmation boxes run red until the rule is met and then green (ten small boxes show the ten characters), and "Buat akun" shows that it is working. `tests/gate-motion-browser.mjs` measures it frame by frame.
- Indonesian confirmation and password-reset emails (`supabase/templates/`) and `scripts/apply-auth-email.mjs` to apply them, with the code settings (6 digits, 10 minutes), to hosted Auth with a backup and a restore. Live text on a solid colour: no images, no hidden preview line, and no link in the code email (the strongest Spam signals for a new sender; Gmail also does not load images for messages it files as spam). The code step says to check Spam or Promosi.
- "Siapkan pot" after the first sign-in (Google or email): name the pot, pick Cabai or Bawang merah, say whether a unit is on hand. Saved on the account (Auth user metadata, validated on read) and opens that crop's journey; with a unit it continues to activation. Checked by `tests/pot-setup-browser.mjs` against a mocked Auth.
- Landing numbers and soil scenes animate what the device does (roots drawing in, LED waves, data up the probes, sunlight onto the panel) instead of naming its parts again; parts are labelled only in the product scene.
- Simulator gate (`#masuk`): sign in with Google or email, or continue as a guest whose data stays in the browser. Deep links pass through the gate and continue to the requested page. Without a backend, the gate is skipped.
- Account menu (initials avatar) with Profil & keamanan and Keluar; the pot mascot moved into the pot pill.
- Profile page (`#profil`) with Akun, Keamanan and Produk sections, one at a time.
- Google sign-in through Supabase Auth (authorization code with PKCE S256). It is offered first when Auth enables Google; the email form stays open below it with Masuk and Daftar tabs (no fold since 2026-11-14), and common Auth errors are explained in Indonesian.
- A dedicated sign-in card: the terracotta pot mascot (also the dashboard avatar) beams and reads along the email at the caret, squeezes its eyes shut and peeks now and then for passwords and codes, droops after a failure and sprouts on success. The steps run sign in, register the pot, then 2FA. On narrow cards the mascot sits small above the form.
- Account security: authenticator-app 2FA (TOTP), a 5-minute step-up and a 24-hour hold after security changes before points can be redeemed, and an activity log.
- Transfer confirmation for passwordless (Google) accounts: a fresh authenticator code or a new Google sign-in.
- Hosted backend on Supabase cloud (project `smart-agro`, ap-southeast-1): the same migrations, four edge functions and Auth settings (Google, passwords of 10+ characters, TOTP, email confirmation). `npm run build:cloud` builds against it from `.env.cloud`; that build requires sign-in and offers no guest visit.

- Care journal and sensor history for an owned unit (Ekosistem → Unit & sensor), on the backend contract in `docs/care-history-api.md`: log watering, fertilizing, inspections and notes (a retry reuses its request id, so nothing is saved twice), page back through 31 days, compare the last week with the one before (missing data stays "—"), and download a CSV for up to 31 days. The journal never changes readings, pumps or rewards. Checked by `tests/care-history-browser.mjs` against a mocked backend.
- Landing "Tanya mesinnya" section (replaces the prototype card row): pick chili or shallot, slide the soil moisture, and read the advice, reason and trigger straight from the simulator's rule engine with that crop's profile.

- The ecosystem page no longer starts with the long online account panel. The summary and module tabs come first, and signed-in data sits at the top of the matching tab (telemetry, knowledge, energy and rewards).

### Added (Miro diagram gap, stages 6–12)
- Suspicious energy claims (at least 90% of the meter limit) are held for review; owners appeal, operators take and decide cases with a reason, and an upheld case credits points once (RW-04).
- Ownership recovery with a statement and evidence references; an operator's approval moves ownership atomically with an outbox proof and a security event for the previous owner (ID-03).
- Units can be renamed, archived and restored; devices are placed on units with history, and ingest rejects packets for another or an archived unit (APP-02).
- "Kondisi unit": latest reading per sensor and alerts for no data, stale data, failed sensors, revoked devices, energy deficit and low battery (APP-03).
- Products are provisioned from a manifest signed by a pinned issuer key (`product-provision` edge function); the public check verifies the Ed25519 signature again in the browser (ID-01, ID-04).
- Password reset (Auth recover with PKCE) and one-time 2FA recovery codes (`mfa-recovery` edge function) (APP-01).
- Chain proof worker (`scripts/chain-worker.mjs`): leased jobs, JSON-RPC send, receipts matched on chain, signer, destination and hash (A08).
- Operator workspace (Profil → Operator): reward cases, ownership recoveries, registry (provision, key rotation, revocation) and chain proofs.
- Paged sensor history for a date range next to the CSV download (APP-06).

## [1.11.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added
- Record proof (UC-12/13/17): freeze a session as exact JSON bytes, record its SHA-256 through the user's wallet (EIP-6963/1193) as a zero-value self-transfer on Base Sepolia or Sepolia, and verify a record against its separate proof file through the network's public node.
- Product QR: camera and photo scanning with a self-hosted ZXing WebAssembly reader, printable SVG/PNG codes, and a public registry status check (`product_public_status`) before sign-in.
- Ownership transfer (ID-03): re-authenticated owners create a one-time 128-bit code (hash stored, 24 h, five attempts); acceptance ends the old ownership and starts the new one in one transaction with an outbox commitment.
- Optional AI wording for owner explanations through any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (locally 9router with a light Antigravity model). Output is schema-checked and filtered; failures keep the rule text, labelled with the reason.
- Mascot settles against sensor noise: worse conditions show at once, easing needs two agreeing evaluations; a fourth "alert" face.
- `CHANGELOG.md`, `SECURITY.md`.

### Changed
- Dashboard rebuilt: plant stage with root-zone lens, trend, care and session-clock cards, rail navigation, Phosphor icons, General Sans and Fraunces, and a rewritten stylesheet.
- Page changes use a 180 ms cross-fade without movement; the landing hero trail now dissolves into a pixel mosaic of the video.
- Online product forms use the Radix select instead of native `<select>`.
- The explanation snapshot schema accepts `ai_explained` only with a model, prompt version and bounded text.

### Fixed
- A missing field in an AI snapshot could pass the database check as NULL; the check now treats NULL as invalid.
- The segmented control pill drifted when a dialog resized.
- The energy browser test reused a fixed device key and could not run twice on one database.
- The local gateway now serves `.wasm`, `.mp4`, `.avif` and `.md` with their content types.

### Removed
- Lucide icons, Nunito Sans, and the unused `public/plants` images.
